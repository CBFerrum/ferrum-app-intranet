# Provenance

This app's design and code are its own. No code was copied from another project.

- Its shape follows what company intranets commonly offer: spaces for teams and topics with their
  members, pages and links, and announcements to everyone or to a team, which readers acknowledge
  when asked.
- The pages themselves are Ferrum Docs' documents in a Drive folder; the wikis kept in this
  workspace's references (Outline, Docmost) were read for what a space holds, not for code.
